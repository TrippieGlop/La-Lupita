Accessibility

Last updated November 9, 2026

We want everyone to be able to enjoy La Lupita, both in the restaurant and online.

In the restaurant

Our restaurant has a wheelchair accessible entrance, wheelchair accessible seating and a wheelchair accessible restroom. Accessible parking is available nearby. If there's anything we can do to make your visit easier, just let us know when you call or when you arrive.

On this website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. Here's some of what we've done:

  • Text and buttons are designed with strong color contrast.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with visible focus outlines and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Headings, landmarks and labels are structured so screen readers can navigate easily.
  • The menu can be searched, and the full menu is available as plain text instead of an image.
  • Animations are turned off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages resize and reflow for phones, tablets and zoomed-in browsers without horizontal scrolling.

Known limitations

The optional Google Map is provided by Google and may not be fully accessible. Our address, directions link and phone number are always available as plain text on the same page.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need the menu in another format, please call us at (610) 868-5733. We'll be happy to read the menu to you, answer questions about ingredients or take your order over the phone. We take accessibility feedback seriously and will work to fix any issues you report.
