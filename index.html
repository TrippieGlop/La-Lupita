Nuestra casa

Bienvenidos a La Lupita

Since 2007 we've been cooking the food we grew up on, right here on the South Side.

Our sauces are made in house, from the deep, dark mole to the bright green tomatillo salsa and the creamy poblano. Tamales come wrapped in corn husks with mole on the side, the pozole is loaded with pork, hominy and chile piquín, and the tacos come on corn tortillas with just cilantro and onion, the way you'd get them back home.

Lehigh students, families on a Sunday, folks on their lunch break, neighbors who've been coming since day one. Everybody ends up at the same tables under the papel picado. Pull up a chair. We'll bring the chips.

¡Buen provecho!
